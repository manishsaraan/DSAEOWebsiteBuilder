Frequently asked questions

What should I include when I contact SortedLaunch?

A link to the repo or the deployed app if one exists, a short description of what it should do, and what is going wrong or what you want built. If you have a deadline or a budget range, include it. That is enough for me to come back with questions or a scoping call slot.

How quickly will I hear back?

I read every message personally and reply by email. If the project is a fit, the next step is a scoping call that takes 2 to 3 days and ends with a fixed quote and a launch date.

Do you sign an NDA before looking at my code?

Yes, if you need one. Send yours with the message. Code access can be a read-only invite to a private repository.

Which time zones do you work with?

I am based in India. Most of the work is asynchronous, with a demo every Friday, so a time-zone gap rarely slows a project down.
