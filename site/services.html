Services and pricing

AI app development agency alternative: one senior engineer, fixed prices

SortedLaunch offers what founders usually look for in an AI app development agency, delivered by one senior engineer based in India working with US, UK and European clients. Three services: repairing vibe coded apps, building AI products and automation in 2 to 4 weeks, and building SaaS MVPs in 3 to 6 weeks. Full projects start at $3,500 fixed.

Service 1

Fix a vibe coded app

For founders who built an app with Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, v0 or Replit and hit the point where the AI can no longer fix its own bugs. I read the generated code, repair what is broken, close the security holes, and leave you with something a human engineer can maintain.

What I check first

  • Authentication and row-level security on every table
  • Secrets and API keys exposed in the client bundle
  • Stripe webhooks, subscription state, and refund paths
  • Database schema, migrations, and backups
  • Error handling around every third-party call

What you get

  • A written audit with severity for each finding
  • Fixes applied to your repo in reviewable pull requests
  • Deployment with environments, logs, and monitoring
  • A short handover document in plain language

Timeline

Audit inside the scoping call, 2 to 3 days. Most repairs fit a two-week pilot sprint. If the app also needs new features, the four-week build follows.

Service 2, 2 to 4 weeks

AI products, features and workflow automation

Two shapes of work. The first is AI inside your product: chat over your own data, document extraction, classification, generation, and agents that take actions. The second is AI across your operations: pipelines that remove manual steps from support, onboarding, fulfilment, or reporting.

AI features in your SaaS

Retrieval over your customers' documents, structured extraction from messy inputs, and assistants that call your own APIs. Built with evaluation sets so you can measure whether the model is doing its job before customers do.

Operations automation

For Blookery, a surprise-trip company, I built an automation pipeline that cut manual workload by 75%. It has processed 500+ trips since launch. The method is in this guide.

Mobile apps and Chrome extensions

Mobile apps take about 4 weeks. Chrome extensions take 2 to 3 weeks. Both follow the same scoping and weekly demo process.

Service 3, 3 to 6 weeks

SaaS MVP development

A web application with authentication, billing, a dashboard, and the one workflow that proves your idea, built to launch in 3 to 6 weeks. I have launched 50+ MVPs, and the products behind them have secured $3M+ in funding. The code is structured so the next engineer, or you, can keep building on it.

Included in every MVP

  • Auth, roles, and account management
  • Stripe subscriptions or one-time payments
  • Admin and user dashboards
  • Transactional email and basic analytics
  • Deployment, environments, and backups

What makes it extendable

Typed code, a migrations-based schema, environment-based configuration, and a README that explains how to run it. If you later hire a team, they start from a working codebase instead of a prototype.

How does the process work?

The same four stages apply to repair work, AI features, and MVPs. You know the price and the launch date before any code is written.

  1. Scoping call, 2 to 3 days

    I read your code or your brief, ask the questions that change the estimate, and send a fixed quote with a launch date.

  2. Pilot sprint, 2 weeks

    A bounded first engagement with a real deliverable. For repairs, this is often the whole job.

  3. Build, 4 weeks

    A demo every Friday. You can change direction early instead of discovering a problem at the end.

  4. Launch plus 2 weeks of fixes

    Launch is included. Anything that breaks in the first two weeks after launch is fixed at no extra cost.

Frequently asked questions

What does an AI app development agency charge compared with SortedLaunch?

Agency quotes for an MVP are priced for a project manager, a designer, and several developers. SortedLaunch full projects start at $3,500 at a fixed price agreed before work starts, because one senior engineer does the work directly. The scoping call gives you an exact number for your scope.

What is included in the $3,500 full project price?

A fixed scope agreed in the scoping call, the build itself with a demo every Friday, launch to your hosting, and two weeks of post-launch fixes. Larger scopes cost more, but the number is fixed before work starts and does not change unless you change the scope.

How does the $3,000 per month option work?

Regular Work is a monthly arrangement for founders who have ongoing engineering needs: new features, fixes, integrations, and the occasional fire. You can pause or cancel anytime. It suits teams that want a senior engineer on call without hiring one full time.

Can you fix an app that was built by another agency or freelancer?

Yes. Taking over code written by someone else is a large share of what I do, whether it came from an agency, a freelancer, or an AI tool. The scoping call includes reading the existing codebase so the quote reflects its real state.

Which tech stack do you use?

I work in the stack your app is already on rather than forcing a migration. If you are starting fresh, the scoping call covers which tools fit. My own writing on manishsaraan.com covers Supabase and Vercel hosting, which is where many of my recent builds live.

Not sure which service fits?

Send the repo, the URL, or the idea. The scoping call sorts out whether you need a repair, a feature, or a full build, and gives you a fixed number either way.
