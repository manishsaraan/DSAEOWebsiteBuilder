What does it take to fix a vibe coded app?

Most AI-generated apps need four things before they can take real customers: working authentication and row-level data rules, a database schema that will not corrupt under load, error handling around payments and third-party APIs, and a deployment with backups and logs. A rewrite is rarely the answer. Targeted repair on the existing codebase is faster and cheaper, and it keeps the parts that already work.

Security and data access

Generated apps often ship with public database tables, API keys in the browser bundle, and no rate limits. I lock those down first, because this is where founders get hurt.

Payments and billing

Stripe webhooks that silently fail, subscriptions that never cancel, and trial logic that lives only in the frontend. I make billing match what the customer was promised.

Reliability and deployment

Proper environments, migrations, logging, monitoring, and backups. Afterwards you can ship changes without holding your breath.

Handover you can read

Every fix comes with a short written explanation of what was wrong, what changed, and how to avoid it next time. You keep control of your own product.

Frequently asked questions

What is a vibe coded app?

A vibe coded app is software built mostly by prompting AI tools such as Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, v0 or Replit, usually by a founder without a full engineering background. The apps often work well as demos. Problems show up when real users, payments, and edge cases arrive and nobody on the team can read the generated code with confidence.

Can you fix a vibe coded app without rebuilding it?

Usually, yes. Most AI-generated apps need targeted repair rather than a rewrite: fixing auth and data access rules, cleaning up the database schema, adding error handling, and setting up deployment and backups. I recommend a rewrite only when the generated code cannot be made safe or maintainable at a reasonable cost, and I say so in the scoping call.

How long does it take to fix a vibe coded app?

A scoping call takes 2 to 3 days and ends with a fixed quote and a launch date. Repair work on an existing app typically fits in a two-week pilot sprint, with a longer build of up to four weeks when new features are added. You see a demo every Friday.

How much does it cost to fix a vibe coded app?

Full projects start at $3,500 at a fixed price agreed before work starts. Ongoing work runs at $3,000 per month and you can pause or cancel anytime. The scoping call tells you which option fits before you commit any money.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. SortedLaunch is remote and works mainly with founders in the US, UK and Europe. I overlap with US and European working hours for calls, and every week ends with a recorded demo you can watch on your own schedule.
