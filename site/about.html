About

About Manish Saraan and SortedLaunch

SortedLaunch is Manish Saraan: one senior engineer based in India, working remotely with founders in the US, UK and Europe. After 10+ years of engineering and 50+ MVPs launched, including a stint running a 23-person agency, the practice now builds and repairs software for founders, 1-on-1, at a fixed price.

Why one engineer instead of a team?

I ran Artzen.io, an agency that grew to 23 people. Agencies are good at volume. They are less good at the thing early-stage founders need most, which is a senior person who understands the whole product and answers the phone. Every layer between the founder and the engineer adds cost, delay, and a chance for the requirement to get lost.

So I removed the layers. SortedLaunch is me. You describe the problem, I read the code, I give you a fixed quote, and I do the work.

The trade-off is capacity. I take on a limited number of projects at a time, and I turn work down when the fit is wrong. The upside is that the engineer with 10+ years of experience is the one touching your codebase, not reviewing someone else's pull request once a week.

What I have built

Across 50+ MVPs, the products I have helped launch have secured $3M+ in client funding, and I hold 30+ long-term retainers. Three documented examples:

  • BlinkIn, AI video platform. I led the frontend architecture. The product secured EUR 2.5M in funding, and an optimized WebRTC implementation brought average call latency to 50ms. Case study.
  • Blookery, surprise-trip engine. I built an automation pipeline that cut manual workload by 75% and has processed 500+ trips since launch. The Blookery CEO has worked with me for the past three years. Case study.
  • LiveEasy, home valuation. I rebuilt the valuation feature, which produced a 5% revenue boost. Case study.

Clients who have shared testimonials include the CEO of Blookery, the CEO of BlinkIn, the co-founder of Aventux, the CTO of KnovaTek Inc and the founder of Trackita. Their words are on manishsaraan.com.

Open source and writing

My GitHub profile has 552 stars across 135 repositories. The project most people know is email-validator, an npm module for checking email syntax with 449 stars and 78 forks. I also write technical posts on manishsaraan.com, such as how to self-host Supabase with Coolify and why I migrated file hosting from Supabase to Vercel Blob. The articles on this site cover repairing AI-generated apps and automating SaaS operations.

How I work with founders

Every engagement starts with a scoping call that takes 2 to 3 days and ends with a fixed quote and a launch date. Then a two-week pilot sprint, then a four-week build with a demo every Friday if the project needs it. Launch is included, with two weeks of post-launch fixes. Pricing is on the services page: full projects from $3,500, or $3,000 per month for ongoing work that you can pause or cancel anytime.

I am based in India. Most communication happens asynchronously in writing, with a demo every Friday, which suits founders who are running a company and do not want to sit in status meetings.
