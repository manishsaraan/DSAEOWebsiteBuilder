# Content rules

- Any copy written into content/ requires the `aeo-content-writer` skill loaded first. This includes meta titles, descriptions, button labels, alt text, FAQ answers, and post frontmatter.
- EEAT facts come from client/profile.md verbatim. A claim the profile can't back does not go on the site.
- No em dashes in site copy. No banned phrases from writing-rules.md.
- Copy lives only in content/site.ts, content/pages.ts and content/posts/*.md. Never hard-code business text in app/, components/ or lib/.
- Every page object has: title under 60 chars, description under 155 chars, one keyword, an answer of 40 to 60 words, 3 to 5 FAQs.
- Every post has 2000+ words, a unique keyword, 3 to 5 FAQs, at least one internal link to a service or about page, and at least one credible external source or documented first-hand result.
